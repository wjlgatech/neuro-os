# SMART Actionables

## 1. Mechanism Extraction
Specific:
- Extract one mechanism card per paper.

Measurable:
- 20 mechanism cards by Day 40.

Actionable:
- Capture:
  - mechanism
  - invariant
  - failure mode
  - prediction

Relevant:
- Supports world-model construction.

Time-bound:
- 1 paper/day maximum.

---

## 2. Deep Continuity
Specific:
- Stay on one thesis for 40 days.

Measurable:
- ≥35 continuity days.

Actionable:
- Every note must answer:
  “How does this improve the thesis?”

Relevant:
- Prevents epistemic fragmentation.

Time-bound:
- Daily review.

---

## 3. Predictive Understanding
Specific:
- Generate predictions from every paper.

Measurable:
- 60 predictions by Day 40.

Actionable:
- Predict:
  - consequences
  - failure conditions
  - observable signals

Relevant:
- Converts passive knowledge into understanding.

Time-bound:
- After each paper.

---

## 4. Assumption Analysis
Specific:
- Extract hidden assumptions.

Measurable:
- ≥3 assumptions/paper.

Actionable:
- Log:
  - assumptions
  - constraints
  - invalidation conditions

Relevant:
- Improves epistemic rigor.

Time-bound:
- Per reading session.

---

## 5. Experimental Thinking
Specific:
- Run one experiment/week.

Measurable:
- ≥5 experiments by Day 40.

Actionable:
- Create:
  - simulation
  - thought experiment
  - implementation prototype
  - falsifiable test

Relevant:
- Grounds theory in reality.

Time-bound:
- Weekly.

# Acceptance Criteria

By Day 40:
- One stable ontology exists.
- One thesis refined through 20+ iterations.
- Contradictions explicitly tracked.
- Predictions logged and reviewed.
- At least 5 experiments executed.
- Cross-domain transfer learning observed.
- Research continuity maintained.
- Mechanism recall measurably improved.
